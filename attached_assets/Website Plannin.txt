Business Description:
Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a cozy, locally loved bakery offering a range of freshly baked goods including cakes, cupcakes, cookies, artisanal breads, and pastries. Known for their customized designer cakes for birthdays, weddings, and events, they also serve coffee and quick snacks. Their focus is on using fresh, high-quality ingredients and maintaining a homely taste in every product.

2. Website Goals

Build an online presence to attract more local customers.

Showcase their variety of products and customized cake designs.

Enable visitors to send inquiries for custom orders or bookings.

Share customer testimonials and success stories.

Display bakery location and make it easier for customers to visit.



---

3. Target Audience

Local residents of Beawar and surrounding towns.

Youth looking for trendy cakes and pastries.

Families planning birthday parties, weddings, anniversaries.

Small businesses or cafes interested in bulk orders.



---

4. Features Required

Home Page: Introduction with featured images of top products.

About Us Page: Story behind the bakery and team introduction.

Products Page:

Cakes (Designer Cakes, Wedding Cakes)

Breads

Pastries

Cookies

Seasonal Specials


Gallery: High-quality images of bakery products and celebrations.

Testimonials: Customer reviews with ratings.

Contact Form: To allow customers to submit inquiries easily.

Google Maps Integration: Showing bakery location.

Social Media Links: Instagram and Facebook pages.

5. Design Preferences

Color Theme:

Pastel Pink (#FFD1DC)

Cream (#FFF8E7)

Light Brown (#C8A165)


Typography:

Primary Font: 'Poppins' for headers (Friendly and clean)

Body Font: 'Roboto' for text (Simple and readable)

Warm, welcoming, and joyful.

Smooth animations (like hover effects on images).

6. Branding

Logo Available: Yes (Cupcake with 'Sweet Crumbs' written stylishly).

Brand Voice: Sweet, friendly, inviting.

Preferred Tone: Homely and approachable rather than corporate.

7. Tools Planned to Use

HTML5 for webpage structure.

Summary

The website for Sweet Crumbs Bakery will serve as an attractive digital portfolio to showcase their products, share customer experiences, and allow potential customers to easily inquire about customized orders. It will use pastel colors, a welcoming design, and simple navigation, ensuring an excellent browsing experience on both desktop and mobile devices.
