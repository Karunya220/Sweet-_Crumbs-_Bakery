About Us

The story behind Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Our Story

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was born out of Mrs. Radhika Soni's passion for baking. What started as a hobby in her home kitchen in 2019 soon became a beloved local business.

After years of baking for family and friends and perfecting her recipes, Radhika decided to follow her dream and open a bakery in 2021. With the support of her family, Sweet Crumbs was established in the heart of Beawar.

The name "Sweet Crumbs" was inspired by the joy of savoring every last crumb of a delicious treat – something Radhika noticed her children doing when they enjoyed her baked goods.

Today, Sweet Crumbs has become a go-to destination for locals seeking quality baked goods made with love and the finest ingredients. Our commitment to maintaining the homely taste in every product remains as strong as ever.
